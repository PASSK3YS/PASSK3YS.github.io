I've been playing Cyberpunk 2077 on my Xbox Series X for the past couple of days and so far I'm loving the game. Night City and the open world as a whole is just breathtaking. CD Project Red have put a lot of love and care into details with this game. The world just feels alive. I'm not even lying, I played Cyberpunk 2077 for about 12 hours straight on the release day. I was hooked and I still am. This is the first game in a while where I just don't want to stop playing and when I do stop playing, I can't stop thinking about the next time I can jump back on the game.

Er bod gan Cyberpunk 2077 ei ddiffygion gyda bygiau bach a gêm gwpl yn torri (yr wyf wedi'i brofi), rhaid imi ddweud bod y gêm hon yn gampwaith o safbwynt stori a phersbectif gweledol. Wrth chwarae trwy'r gêm, rydw i wedi bod yn manteisio ar y modd llun yn y gêm trwy gymryd dwsinau o ergydion sgrin.
