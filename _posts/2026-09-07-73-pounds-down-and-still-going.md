At my heaviest, I weighed 322 pounds. Today, I weigh 249 pounds. That's 73 pounds gone, and I've lost 6 inches off my waist along the way. My next milestone is 200 pounds, and while there's still a long road ahead, I wanted to share how I got here.

Two weeks ago, I started intermittent fasting using the 16:8 method. That means a 16 hour fast with an 8 hour eating window. It sounds brutal on paper, but the structure has honestly made things simpler. Instead of grazing all day, I know exactly when I eat and when I don't.

Fasting isn't the only change I've made. I've cut down on sugary foods and drinks, and that alone has made a noticeable difference. I'm also drinking a lot more water than I used to, which keeps me hydrated and helps curb the urge to snack. On top of that, I'm walking a lot more than I ever did before, and those extra steps have really added up.

I won't pretend any of this has been easy. My biggest struggle is that I've always been a nighttime eater. Those late night cravings hit hard, and breaking a habit I've had for years has been tough. But I'm pressing through. Some days are harder than others, but every day I stick with it feels like a win.

Looking ahead, I'm considering taking things further with water only fasts. My plan is to start small with one full day of no eating, just drinking water. If that goes well, I'd progress to a 2 day water fast. It's an experiment, and I'll listen to my body as I go.

This journey is far from over. There's still distance between me and my goal, but the results speak for themselves: 73 pounds down, 6 inches off my waist, and a whole lot more confidence than when I started.
