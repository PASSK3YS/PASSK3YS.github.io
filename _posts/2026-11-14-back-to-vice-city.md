The wait is almost over... Just under a week to go, and we'll all be in Vice City.

Last week, I finally pre-ordered the Ultimate Edition of GTA 6 on XBOX. It's crazy to think that the game has already made over $1 billion in pre-orders alone! Anyways, I had been saving up Microsoft Reward points for almost a year, so most of the cost of the game was covered. I only had to pay £40 to pre-order.

![GTA VI on XBOX](https://noted.do/m/uploads/u28/2026/11/1d5f816c02f395ca.jpg "I've cleared out space on my console and pre loaded GTA 6 ready for November 19th.")

It's crazy to think that 13 years ago, I was standing outside my local game store at a midnight launch to pick up my copy of GTA 5 on the XBOX 360. Back then, I never would have guessed it would be over a decade before getting the next installment in the Grand Theft Auto franchise. I'm pretty sure I'm classed as a boomer now by the younger folk, as it's been that long since a new GTA has come out!

Reading the recent press release from Rockstar Games about GTA 6 being locked at 30FPS with no option for a 60FPS performance mode is a bit disappointing, but I still have high hopes that the game will be really good regardless.
