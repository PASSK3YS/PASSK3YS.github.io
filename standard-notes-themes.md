## Standard Notes Themes

**All of my themes are published under the [GPL-3.0 License](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.en.html).**

### Standard Blue
A light blue theme for Standard Notes.

![Standard Blue Preview](https://files.horizon.pics/becc01e8-60e7-4b1c-b278-27ed17765201?a=480&region=eu-central&mime1=image&mime2=jpeg)

To install, go to **Preferences > Plugins > Install Custom Plugin**

Paste the following link in the text box:
`https://listed.to/p/gWG2LYgCPH`

Alternatively, this theme is now included in the official Standard Notes Community Plugins repository.

GitHub: [PASSK3YS/standard-blue](https://github.com/PASSK3YS/standard-blue)
