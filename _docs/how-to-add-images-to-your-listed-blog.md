# How to add images to your Listed.to blog post

I get many people asking me how I am able to add images to some of my blog posts on Listed.to. Believe it or not, it's actually very simple to add images to your own Listed blog posts. I'll show you how to do it in this How to guide.

---

**1:** You need to sign up to a third-party image hosting website. 

*(As Listed.to doesn't have first party image support from Standard Notes, you will need a third-party image hosting site for your images)*

I have listed a few image hosting sites that I've used in the past:

*(Note: These are recommendations, but you can use whatever hosting provider you'd like)*

[Vgy.me](https://vgy.me)

[ImageBB](https://imgbb.com/)

[Horizon](https://horizon.pics)

**2:** Once you've made an account on your chosen image hosting website, upload the images you want to include in your blog post on Listed.

**3:** In Standard Notes, open the note that you want to publish on Listed.to. In the editor on the note you've opened, insert an image tag with the link to your image. 

Copy and paste the following into the note:

`![](https://example.com/image.jpg)`

**4:** Copy the **CDN URL** from the image hosting provider and replace the example URL in the image tag.

*For the best results, make sure to use a markdown editor so you can split the page to see if the image tag has worked.*

**5:** Save your note and sync it with Listed.to.

**6:** Check your blog post on Listed.to. The image should now display in your Listed.to blog post!
