category: Standard Notes

```markdown
## Morning:
Today's Priority: [The single most important task you need to complete today]

Gratitude: [One to three things you are genuinely thankful for right now]

Daily Intention: [How you want to show up today, e.g., patient, focused, energized]

## Midday:
[Use this space to capture stray thoughts, sudden ideas, or frustrations]

[Write down quick reminders or notes from conversations]

[Jot down anything that is distracting you so you can let it go]

## Evening:
Biggest Win: [A moment, task, or interaction that went really well]

Challenge Encountered: [An obstacle you faced and how you handled it]

Lesson Learned: [A new realization or piece of knowledge gained today]

Tomorrow's Adjustment: [One small thing you want to do differently or better tomorrow]
```
